# Webcam faces

Turn your webcam on with I or the office menu. Other nearby players see its picture on your avatar's face.

The face uses the middle 50% of the webcam's square crop, keeping the original aspect ratio and reducing background around your face. Your own preview is mirrored; other people see the normal orientation. Keep your face near the center of your camera frame. This is a fixed centered crop and does not automatically track faces.

Webcam permission, proximity limits and stream sharing remain unchanged.
